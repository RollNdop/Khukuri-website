Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, and how it's used.

Last updated: September 2026

What we don't collect

This site has no sign-ups, accounts, or order forms. Contacting us happens through direct links — email, phone, and WhatsApp — which open your own device's apps rather than sending information through this website.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit this site and which pages they look at. It uses cookies to do this. This data is anonymized and aggregated — we see overall visit patterns, not who any individual visitor is.

You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using Google's official opt-out browser extension.

Other Google services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays a map from Google Maps on the Contact page. Loading either means your browser contacts Google's servers, which may log standard technical information like your IP address, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

WhatsApp

Links on this site that open WhatsApp connect you to a service operated by Meta. Messages sent there are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

Cookies, summarized

The only cookies set on this site come from Google Analytics, as described above. We don't use cookies for advertising, and we don't sell or share data with third parties beyond what's described here.
