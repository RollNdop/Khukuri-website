Our approach

Every khukuri here is made to be used, not just displayed. That means real high-carbon steel, a properly hardened and tempered edge, and a handle fitted to actually hold up over years of use — whether it ends up on a wall, in a kitchen, or in a backpack.

Nothing is stamped from a mold. Small differences from one piece to the next aren't a flaw — they're the sign that a person made it.
