Made by hand, not by mold

This workshop has been shaping khukuris in Dharan for generations, one blade at a time. Every piece that leaves here is still hand-forged, hand-tempered, and hand-fitted — no factories, no shortcuts.

What you're holding isn't mass-produced. It's made by someone who has spent a lifetime learning exactly how a khukuri should feel in the hand — its balance, its edge, its weight.
